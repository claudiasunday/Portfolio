I'm Claudia Sunday a product designer and much more . I'm from Barcelona

My latest experience at Dogfy Diet highlights my versatility: I've designed both customer-facing products (the mobile App and client area) and internal business tools (the sales team CRM). My approach combines data-driven decisions via A/B testing with technical rigor — maintaining robust Design Systems and collaborating closely with IT and Marketing to ensure flawless product implementation.

I have a strong aptitude for early-stage product design, where I thoughtfully apply customer research to craft impactful MVPs.

I've also collaborated with front-end teams, particularly in developing Design Systems, bridging user needs with developer efficiency. My ultimate goal is to design products that simplify life for users and developers. With a curious and proactive mindset, I thrive on turning challenges into actionable, user-centered solutions.
